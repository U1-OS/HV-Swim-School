Accessibility

Swimming should feel welcoming. Getting information should, too.

Updated 13 September 2026

We aim to make this website usable with a keyboard, on a phone, at enlarged text sizes and with assistive technology. It includes labelled forms, a skip link, visible keyboard focus and controls to pause decorative motion.

Your preferences

The website respects your device’s reduced-motion setting. You can also use the motion control to pause animation. Browser zoom and text-size settings remain available. Optional Facebook content can be left unloaded.

Help with an enquiry

If a form, document or page is difficult to use, call 0413 462 112. Tell us what you need, the page involved and, if you are comfortable sharing it, the device or assistive technology you use. We can discuss your lesson enquiry directly.

Testing and improvement

Local checks cover keyboard navigation, form labels, responsive layouts and selected security and functional behaviour. An independent accessibility review and live screen-reader testing remain part of launch preparation. We do not claim certified WCAG conformance.
