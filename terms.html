Terms & cancellations

Clear information for families, visitors and the HV Swim community.

Updated 13 September 2026 · Prepared for review before launch

These terms are issued by HVS BENDIGO PTY LTD (ABN 46 687 937 962), trading as HV Swim Bendigo. They explain website use and the proposed lesson arrangements. Creating or using a family account does not itself confirm enrolment or acceptance of a particular class, fee or lesson agreement. Lesson terms must be provided and agreed before enrolment.

Enquiries and enrolment

Submitting the enquiry form starts a conversation — it is not a booking and does not hold a place. A place is confirmed only when we confirm it to you and any required payment is made.

General questions can be sent through the enquiry form. Signed-in families can use their support workspace. These services are asynchronous and are not monitored as emergency channels. A reference confirms receipt, not external email delivery.

You must be a parent or legal guardian, or have their authority, to enrol a swimmer. The information you give us at enrolment must be accurate, particularly emergency contacts and anything affecting safe participation in the water. Please tell us promptly if it changes.

Class placement

We place swimmers in the class that suits their current stage, not only their age. If a class turns out to be the wrong fit we will talk to you about moving them. Class sizes are capped so instructors can give real attention; when a class is full, families can ask management about a waitlist. Placement is reviewed for suitability and available class options.

Fees and payment

Published group lessons cost $22.50 each; private tuition is quoted separately and confirmed before you commit. Group lessons are charged for the full school term. The term balance is due when enrolment is confirmed. HV Swim uses Xero for lesson invoicing. Website payment connections are not active in this preview. Merchandise checkout will use Shopify only after the range and checkout service are approved. The invoice or enrolment confirmation will state the available payment method and the total based on the number of scheduled lessons in that term.

If payment is not received when due, HV Swim may hold or release the class place after contacting the family. Payment obligations and consumer rights are not changed by a delay in platform or payment-provider processing.

Cancellations, missed lessons and credits

This section is what most families want to find quickly, so we have kept it in plain language.

If your swimmer is absent

Each swimmer may receive up to two lesson-value account credits per term for absences notified to HV Swim. Credits are recorded against the family account; they are not make-up classes. HV Swim does not provide make-up classes for missed lessons.

If you withdraw from a term

Term enrolment is a commitment for the term. If you cancel or withdraw part-way through a term, the unused term balance is not refunded. This does not limit any remedy you are entitled to under the Australian Consumer Law.

If we cancel a lesson

Occasionally we have to cancel — pool plant failure, unsafe water, extreme weather, or an instructor being unavailable at short notice. When we cancel, we will tell you as early as we can and apply a lesson-value credit or otherwise ensure you are not charged for that cancelled lesson. We do not schedule make-up classes.

Illness and injury

Please keep swimmers out of the water when they are unwell, particularly with gastro symptoms, and follow current public-health advice on how long to wait before returning. Tell us about an injury or condition that affects participation so we can adapt safely or pause lessons.

For an extended illness or injury, contact HV Swim as early as possible so the team can discuss safe participation and the enrolment options available in the circumstances.

Nothing in this section limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. Those rights apply in addition to anything written here and cannot be excluded.

Safety and behaviour

Swimmers must follow instructor directions and pool rules at all times. A parent or guardian must remain on site throughout every lesson, except where the swimmer is attending a Stroke Development class. Parents and guardians must also supervise siblings who are not in a lesson.

We may pause or end a swimmer's enrolment where behaviour puts that swimmer, another swimmer or an instructor at risk. We will always talk to you first unless there is an immediate safety concern.

Ask management for current insurance, instructor qualification and working-with-children-check information. Please keep belongings secure and report lost property to the team. Nothing here excludes responsibility or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Photographs and media

We do not photograph or film a class unless the required clearance has been confirmed for that class and the swimmers who may appear. Individual consent is never assumed. See our photo and media consent for what consent covers and how to change it.

Your account

Keep your sign-in details private and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account. Tell us promptly about unauthorised activity so we can investigate and help protect your account. We may suspend an account being used in a way that threatens the security of the platform or the privacy of other families.

You may create or access a family account through Google or Apple when that option is active. The verified provider email and identity are linked to your HV Swim family account, and your use of the provider remains subject to its own terms. Social sign-up never creates staff or management access; those accounts are issued and approved by HV Swim.

Swimmer achievements and certificates

Achievements are HV Swim progress acknowledgements supported by a family-visible evidence note. Staff may keep a separate private operational note. We may correct or revoke an achievement when it was issued in error or the record needs to be withdrawn; issue and revocation actions are audited. A printable HTML certificate is not a government or industry qualification, licence, AUSTSWIM/SWIM accreditation or guarantee of future performance.

Merchandise

The public family merchandise range shown on our site is currently at concept stage; final prices have not been published and no order can be placed yet. Staff uniform products are available only inside secure staff and management workspaces. Every item must pass artwork-rights, specification, landed-cost, physical-sample, care/returns and checkout tests before it becomes available. When the store opens, approved merchandise sales will be handled through Shopify under its checkout terms, and your rights under the Australian Consumer Law will apply to every purchase.

Supplier and blank-brand names describe possible sourcing routes only. They do not state that HV Swim is a partner, sponsor, endorsed business, licensee or authorised reseller. Branded goods will be offered only where the approved supplier and decoration rights permit it.

Website content

Class times, prices, availability and pool conditions are published in good faith and can change. We will correct errors as soon as we find them. Website material belongs to HV Swim Bendigo or is used under an applicable licence. Third-party rights remain with their owners; permission is required for reuse.

Urgent closure and changed-condition alerts refresh while an active website page is open. They are not guaranteed off-device push or an emergency warning service. External email, SMS or push delivery requires configured providers, applicable consent and explicit approval; it is not active in this preview.

Third-party association names and directory links do not make HV Swim or every instructor accredited by those organisations. Any member/provider badge displayed must be the exact current artwork issued to HV Swim and used within its authorised term; generic, scraped, redrawn or expired marks must not be used.
