Enrolment · Cancellations · Safety

The agreement between us, in plain language.

Enrolment, fees, absence credits, cancellations, safety expectations and your rights.

Effective and last reviewed 30 August 2026 · Review status

These terms are issued by HVS BENDIGO PTY LTD (ABN 46 687 937 962), trading as HV Swim Bendigo. They cover use of the HV Swim Bendigo website and platform, and enrolment in our swimming lessons. By enrolling a swimmer or using your family account, you agree to them.

Enquiries and enrolment

Submitting the enquiry form starts a conversation — it is not a booking and does not hold a place. A place is confirmed only when we confirm it to you and any required payment is made.

“Message HV Swim” creates an asynchronous support ticket and reference in our staff queue. It is not live chat, does not promise a response by a particular time, and must not be used for an emergency. The current build does not send an external support email, so use our published phone number or emergency services where appropriate.

You must be a parent or legal guardian, or have their authority, to enrol a swimmer. The information you give us at enrolment must be accurate, particularly emergency contacts and anything affecting safe participation in the water. Please tell us promptly if it changes.

Class placement

We place swimmers in the class that suits their current stage, not only their age. If a class turns out to be the wrong fit we will talk to you about moving them. Class sizes are capped so instructors can give real attention; when a class is full, families can join a waitlist and are contacted in order as places are released.

Fees and payment

Lessons cost $22.50 each and are charged for the full school term. The term balance is due when enrolment is confirmed. The invoice or enrolment confirmation will state the available payment method and the total based on the number of scheduled lessons in that term.

If payment is not received when due, HV Swim may hold or release the class place after contacting the family. Payment obligations and consumer rights are not changed by a delay in platform or payment-provider processing.

Cancellations, missed lessons and credits

This section is what most families want to find quickly, so we have kept it in plain language.

If your swimmer is absent

Each swimmer may receive up to two lesson-value account credits per term for absences notified to HV Swim. Credits are recorded against the family account; they are not make-up classes. HV Swim does not provide make-up classes for missed lessons.

If you withdraw from a term

Term enrolment is a commitment for the term. If you cancel or withdraw part-way through a term, the unused term balance is not refunded. This does not limit any remedy you are entitled to under the Australian Consumer Law.

If we cancel a lesson

Occasionally we have to cancel — pool plant failure, unsafe water, extreme weather, or an instructor being unavailable at short notice. When we cancel, we will tell you as early as we can and apply a lesson-value credit or otherwise ensure you are not charged for that cancelled lesson. We do not schedule make-up classes.

Illness and injury

Please keep swimmers out of the water when they are unwell, particularly with gastro symptoms, and follow current public-health advice on how long to wait before returning. Tell us about an injury or condition that affects participation so we can adapt safely or pause lessons.

For an extended illness or injury, contact HV Swim as early as possible so the team can discuss safe participation and the enrolment options available in the circumstances.

Nothing in this section limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. Those rights apply in addition to anything written here and cannot be excluded.

Safety and behaviour

Swimmers must follow instructor directions and pool rules at all times. A parent or guardian must remain on site throughout every lesson, except where the swimmer is attending a Stroke Development class. Parents and guardians must also supervise siblings who are not in a lesson.

We may pause or end a swimmer's enrolment where behaviour puts that swimmer, another swimmer or an instructor at risk. We will always talk to you first unless there is an immediate safety concern.

We hold public liability insurance and our instructors hold current qualifications and working-with-children checks. We are not responsible for personal property brought to the venue.

Photographs and media

We do not photograph or film a class unless the required clearance has been confirmed for that class and the swimmers who may appear. Individual consent is never assumed. See our photo and media consent for what consent covers and how to change it.

Your account

Keep your sign-in details private and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account. You are responsible for activity under your account. We may suspend an account being used in a way that threatens the security of the platform or the privacy of other families.

Swimmer achievements and certificates

Achievements are HV Swim progress acknowledgements supported by a family-visible evidence note. Staff may keep a separate private operational note. We may correct or revoke an achievement when it was issued in error or the record needs to be withdrawn; issue and revocation actions are audited. A printable HTML certificate is not a government or industry qualification, licence, AUSTSWIM/SWIM accreditation or guarantee of future performance.

Urgent closure and changed-condition alerts refresh while an active website or connected-app page is open. They are not guaranteed off-device push or an emergency warning service.
