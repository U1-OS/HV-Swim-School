Product confidence

Size it, care for it, keep it longer.

Final measurements will be published after samples are approved. This guide sets the standard the live store will follow.

01

Measure before ordering

Use chest, waist and torso measurements against the final supplier chart. For growing swimmers, choose comfort without loose fabric that can drag in the water.

Final garment chart pending samples
02

Protect pool fabrics

Rinse swimwear and rash garments in cool fresh water after every lesson. Dry in shade and avoid bleach, softener, ironing and tumble drying.

Chlorine-resistant samples required
03

Make gear easy to find

Names can be added to selected bottles and bags after the personalisation method is tested for water, rubbing and regular pool-deck cleaning.

Personalisation is not yet live
04

Know the returns rules

The production store will clearly separate standard, personalised and hygiene-sensitive items before checkout, with Australian Consumer Law protections retained.
