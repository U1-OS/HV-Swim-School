Protecting families and staff

The platform checks permissions on the server, restricts family records to the correct account and protects changes against cross-site request forgery. Passwords are hashed; sensitive swimmer notes are encrypted in storage. Protected account responses are not stored in the offline cache. These controls reduce risk; no website can guarantee perfect security.

Help keep your account safe

Use a unique password, keep recovery details current and sign out on shared devices. Never send passwords, recovery codes, card details or private swimmer records through a public enquiry. Staff and management should use the available multifactor authentication and keep access limited to their work.

Report a suspected security issue

Call 0413 462 112 and ask for the person responsible for website security. The business mailbox is awaiting verification, so it is not yet a dependable security-reporting channel. Give a short description, the affected page, the time and a way to contact you. Ask for a secure channel before sending sensitive evidence.

If you encounter someone else’s information, stop, do not copy or share it, and report the page and time. A report does not require you to prove the issue by accessing further records.

Responsible testing

This page is not blanket permission to scan or attack the service. Arrange written authorisation and scope with the owner before testing. Do not access other people’s accounts, run disruptive tests, send unsolicited messages, alter payments, or test third-party services through this website. No bounty or response deadline is promised.

When something goes wrong

Management will assess the issue, restrict affected access where necessary, preserve relevant evidence and work on recovery. Where applicable, affected people and regulators will be notified as required by law. Safety emergencies need emergency services, not a website report.

Privacy and accessibility

For personal-information requests see Privacy. For help using the website see Accessibility.
