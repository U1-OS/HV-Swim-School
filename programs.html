A few helpful answers

Before you begin.

What if my swimmer is nervous?

Tell us when you enquire. We can discuss a calm introduction and whether a small group or private lesson would be a more comfortable starting point.

Can adults learn here too?

Yes. Adults are welcome to enquire about a suitable program or private tuition, whether starting out or improving an existing skill.

Does an enquiry reserve a place?

No. We review your preferences and contact you to confirm suitable options, fees and arrangements before you decide to enrol.
