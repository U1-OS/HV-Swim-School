What should we bring?

A towel, suitable swimwear and a change of clothes are a useful starting point. Ask the team about swim nappies, goggles or anything specific to your swimmer’s class.

Where do we go when we arrive?

Check your confirmed class venue and the location information before travelling. The team can confirm the entrance, arrival time and any individual access arrangements.

Does a parent or carer stay?

A parent or guardian remains onsite except for Stroke Development classes. Infant aquatics involves a parent or carer in the water; confirm the arrangements for your class with the team.

What if my swimmer feels nervous?

Tell us what helps them feel comfortable, including communication preferences or previous water experiences. We can discuss a suitable starting point and how to prepare together.

What happens in the first lesson?

Your instructor helps your swimmer settle in and understand the class routine. The team confirms the appropriate starting level and discusses their next steps.
