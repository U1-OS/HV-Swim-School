Infant aquatics

Warm, positive early water experiences that build familiarity, connection and safer foundations with a trusted adult in the water.

  • Water familiarity through play and routine
  • Supported movement, cues and safe handling
  • Confidence for both swimmer and carer

02Build
Beginner to progressing swimmers · Small groups

Learn to swim

A progressive, skill-based pathway that develops comfort, breath control, body position and increasingly independent movement.

  • Calm entry, recovery and practical water safety
  • Core swimming shapes and breath control
  • Small-group coaching with personal feedback
Lesson fee$22.50Checking timetableAsk about learn to swim
03Develop
Independent swimmers · Technique focused

Stroke development

More efficient technique, stronger movement patterns and growing endurance for swimmers ready to move beyond the foundations.

  • Stroke mechanics and more efficient breathing
  • Body position, turns and stronger coordination
  • Endurance built without losing good technique
Lesson fee$22.50Checking timetableAsk about development
04Personalise
All ages and abilities · One-to-one coaching

Private lessons

Focused tuition shaped around a swimmer's confidence, communication style, learning pace and personal goals.

  • Individual pace, goals and instructor attention
  • A supportive option for confidence or specific skills
  • Available for children, teens and adults
