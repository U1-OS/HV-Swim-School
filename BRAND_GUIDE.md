# HV Swim Bendigo brand guide

V5.10.1 refines the existing HV Swim identity rather than replacing it. The rising sun,
freestyle swimmer, layered wave, navy/cyan/gold palette and `HV SWIM BENDIGO` name remain
the recognisable core.

## Primary palette

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Deep water / primary navy | HV Navy | `#061A3B` |
| Mid-water accent | Ocean Blue | `#008CCB` |
| Bright water accent | HV Cyan | `#19C8F4` |
| Optimism / primary CTA | Warm Gold | `#FFC928` |
| Contrast | White | `#FFFFFF` |

Orange is a small supporting highlight inside the sun only. It is not a primary interface
colour. Public and platform typography uses the locally hosted Manrope variable family;
its SIL Open Font License is stored in `assets/fonts/MANROPE-OFL.txt`.

Use the full logo for the footer, large signs, presentations and larger merchandise
placements. Use the symbol for browser favicons, app icons, avatars and other small spaces.
Interface icons come from the local SVG set rather than operating-system emoji so they stay
visually consistent on macOS, Windows, iOS and Android.

## Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space around the full logo equal to at least the height of the `BENDIGO`
lettering. Do not display the full wordmark below 96 CSS pixels wide; switch to the symbol
instead. Keep the symbol at least 24 CSS pixels wide.

## Correct use

- Use the transparent artwork directly; never add a black rectangle or use blend modes to
  hide a background.
- Preserve aspect ratio, colours and orientation.
- Do not recolour individual waves, redraw the swimmer, add effects or place copy over the
  mark.
- On busy photography, place the mark on a solid navy panel or use the white monochrome
  proof after approval.
- App icons must remain opaque and keep critical artwork inside the mask-safe area.

## Merchandise boundary

These transparent PNGs are suitable for digital proofs and physical sampling. Before bulk
production, have a brand designer manually vectorise and typeset the approved mark into SVG,
EPS and print-ready PDF, define CMYK/spot colours, and obtain a physical print or embroidery
proof. Supplier-created DST/PES embroidery files must be approved from a stitched sample;
they cannot be reliably generated from a website preview.

The premium collection contains 24 planned products. A concept image or catalogue card is
not approval to manufacture: each product must pass the artwork-rights, exact specification,
landed-cost, physical-sample, care/returns and Shopify test-order gates in
`MERCH_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md`. Named suppliers and blank brands are sourcing candidates only.
Never describe HV Swim as their partner, sponsor, licensee or authorised reseller without
current written authority, and use only supplier-authorised product imagery.

## Third-party badges and marks

AUSTSWIM, SWIM Schools Australia and Autism Swim artwork remains owned and controlled by
those organisations. Display only the exact current badge issued to HV Swim after the
membership/provider evidence, authorised wording and expiry/renewal date required by
`ASSOCIATION_BADGE_REQUIREMENTS.md` are recorded. A public directory listing is not permission
to copy a corporate logo, and an industry badge must never be merged into the HV Swim logo.

## Certificates and operational messaging

Printable swimmer certificates are HV Swim progress acknowledgements. They must use the HV
Swim identity, certificate reference and family-visible evidence without suggesting a formal
qualification, licence, AUSTSWIM/SWIM accreditation or provider endorsement. Staff-private
notes never appear on the certificate, and revoked records must no longer present as active.

Use “urgent website alert” for the current active-page closure feed and “message HV Swim”
for the asynchronous support queue. Do not call either feature guaranteed push, emergency
notification or live chat until the relevant provider-backed service is implemented and
approved.

## Image-generation record

The V5.3 primary and symbol artwork was created with the built-in image-generation tool
using the previous supplied logo as the edit target. The brief required the same sun, wave,
swimmer, wording and colour identity, simplified shapes, exact `HV SWIM` / `BENDIGO` text,
transparent alpha, no black background, no mockup and no extra wording. Both selected files
were visually checked on white and navy backgrounds before integration.
