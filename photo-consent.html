Child safety · Consent first

Photos only when you say so.

What consent covers, what we will never do, and how to change your mind at any time.

Last reviewed 30 August 2026 · Review status

We love showing what our swimmers can do — a first float, a first lap, a proud face at the end of a term. We also know that a photograph of a child is personal information, and that some families would rather their swimmer was not photographed at all. Both are completely fine.

We never assume consent. HV Swim does not take photographs or video during a class unless clearance has first been confirmed for that class and the swimmers who may appear. Photo and media consent is recorded for each swimmer individually, is off unless you turn it on, and can be changed or withdrawn at any time.

What consent covers

If you give consent, it means we may take photographs or short video of your swimmer during lessons, events and presentations, and use them for:

  • Our website and the HV Swim Bendigo Facebook page
  • Printed material such as flyers, posters and term newsletters
  • Celebrating milestones and end-of-term achievements

Consent does not extend to anything else. In particular, we will not sell images, pass them to advertisers, or use them in paid advertising without asking you separately.

What we will not do

  • We do not publish a swimmer's surname, school, class time or any location detail alongside a photograph.
  • We do not publish images of swimmers in change rooms or bathrooms, and photography is not permitted in those areas by anyone.
  • We do not tag children in social media posts.
  • We do not use images that a swimmer appears uncomfortable in, whatever consent says.

If a swimmer says they do not want their photo taken on the day, we stop. A child's wishes come first, regardless of what the consent form records.

Changing or withdrawing consent

You can change consent at any time in your family account, or by telling any instructor or emailing us. There is no need to explain why.

When consent is withdrawn we stop using new images immediately and remove existing ones from our website and social media as soon as we reasonably can. Printed material already distributed cannot be recalled, and images may remain in third-party caches or in copies other people have already saved — that is a limit of the internet, not a policy choice.

Photography by other families

Parents and visitors must not photograph or film a class unless an instructor has confirmed that the required class clearance is in place. Any permitted photo must focus on your own swimmer and must not be posted if it contains another family's child without that family's permission. Photography and filming are never permitted in change rooms or bathrooms.

How consent is recorded

Consent is stored against each swimmer in your family account, so you can see its current state at any time. Where a swimmer has more than one parent or guardian, we act on the most restrictive instruction we have been given.

Questions

Talk to any instructor, email bendigo@hvswimschool.com or call 0413 462 112. How we handle personal information generally is covered in our privacy policy.
