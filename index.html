Still have a question?
What age can swimmers start?+

HV Swim's pathway begins from approximately four months with a parent or carer, and continues through children, teens and adults.

Are private lessons available?+

Yes. One-on-one tuition can support specific goals, confidence, communication preferences or a more individual pace.

How do I know which level to choose?+

Use the lesson finder as a starting point. The team will confirm the most suitable program after learning about the swimmer's current confidence and experience.

Where can I see pool conditions?+

The Locations page shows venue access, directions, live local weather and the latest water reading published by HV Swim staff.

Acknowledgement of Country

HV Swim Bendigo acknowledges that our Bendigo venues are on Dja Dja Wurrung Country. We acknowledge and extend our appreciation to the Dja Dja Wurrung People, the Traditional Owners of the land and waters on which we learn, work and gather. We pay our respects to leaders and Elders past, present and future, and recognise their continuing connection to Country, culture and community.
