Australian Privacy Principles

Your information, and how we look after it.

What we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how you can access or correct it.

Last reviewed 30 August 2026 · Review status

HVS BENDIGO PTY LTD (ABN 46 687 937 962), trading as HV Swim Bendigo ("we", "us"), runs swimming lessons in California Gully, Bendigo, Victoria. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website and family, staff and management platform, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how you can access or correct it.

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Paul and Laura Smith are the contacts for privacy questions and complaints. Correspondence can be sent to bendigo@hvswimschool.com or PO Box 154, Wallan VIC 3756.

Information we collect

When you make an enquiry

Our enquiry form does not require an account. It collects the swimmer's first name and age range, their current confidence level and goal, the program and class times you are interested in, and your name, email address, phone number and preferred contact method. There are two optional free-text fields for lesson experience and support needs.

The support-needs field asks for teaching preferences rather than detailed medical history. Please do not enter clinical detail there — if a swimmer has a medical condition we need to know about, we will collect that properly at enrolment.

When you message HV Swim

The support widget collects a topic, your name, email address, optional phone number, subject, message and consent acknowledgement. It also includes an empty anti-spam field. A successful submission creates a reference and an item in the protected staff queue. This is asynchronous, not live chat or an emergency service, and the current build does not send your message through an external email provider.

When you enrol a swimmer

A family account stores your name, email address, phone number and a securely hashed password. We never store your password itself.

If you choose “Continue with Google” or “Continue with Apple”, that provider gives us a verified email address, a provider-specific account identifier and, when available, your name. Apple may give you the option to use a private relay email address. We store the provider name, identifier and verified email so you can return to the same family account. We do not store the provider access token, refresh token, ID token or your Google/Apple password. Staff and management accounts cannot be created or linked this way without HV Swim approval.

For each swimmer we record their name, date of birth, current level, an emergency contact, allergies, lesson-relevant medication, any medical or communication, sensory and learning-support notes needed for safe teaching, and whether photo and media consent has been given. Photo consent is off unless you turn it on.

Date of birth, emergency contacts, allergy, medication and medical notes are collected because we teach children in water and need to place swimmers correctly, respond to incidents and reach a responsible adult quickly. This is health information and receives a higher level of protection under Australian privacy law. We collect it only with your consent and only to the extent it affects safe participation. Families can update these details inside their account; audit records identify which fields changed but do not copy the health information itself into the audit description.

Bookings and lesson history

We record class bookings, cancellations, completed lessons and waitlist positions so we can run classes, manage capacity fairly and show you your own history.

When staff award a swimmer achievement, we record the achievement, family-visible evidence, certificate reference, issuing staff member and date. A separate operational note may be visible only to authorised staff. Families can see active achievements for their own swimmers; issue and revocation actions, including the reason for revocation, are retained in the audit record.

If an incident or injury is reported, we record the child and family numbers, reporting worker number, time, venue, objective account of what happened, observed injury or symptoms, first aid, follow-up, witnesses and whether a parent was notified. Incident details are encrypted in the database and are available only to authorised staff, management and the involved family. Audit records contain the reference and routing details, not the sensitive narrative.

If you work for us

For staff we hold a permanent HV worker number, rosters, clock-on and clock-off timestamps, break records, manually entered hours, approved timesheets and certificate/licence documents with expiry dates. These records support payroll, supervision, first-aid and child-safety obligations.

Workers select their work location when clocking on. The platform does not request GPS coordinates, background location or continuous device tracking. Authorised management may map a worker record to the matching Xero employee/payroll identifiers and, where needed for private uniform orders, a Shopify customer identifier.

Automatically collected information

When someone signs in with email and password, we log the email address used, the IP address it came from and whether the attempt succeeded. These records are automatically deleted after 30 days. A Google or Apple sign-in start temporarily stores a one-way digest of the security state, the IP address and replay-protection values for up to 10 minutes. This protects accounts against guessing, flooding and sign-in replay attacks.

Our public pages do not use advertising or third-party analytics trackers. The homepage includes an optional Facebook timeline. It does not connect to Meta until you select “Load Facebook posts”. If you load it, Meta may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device details, and may use cookies under its own privacy terms.

Urgent pool alerts and optional lesson reminders currently refresh while the website or signed-in family portal is open. Email, SMS and delivery to a closed browser are not active. If HV Swim later enables an external reminder or push provider, the service will require consent and relevant delivery identifiers; this policy and the provider list will be updated before those identifiers are collected.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to enquiries and match swimmers to suitable classes
  • To run lessons safely, including responding to incidents
  • To manage bookings, waitlists, cancellations and payments
  • To roster staff, record hours and meet employment and payroll obligations
  • To send information about your own lessons, classes and account
  • To respond to support tickets, record swimmer progress achievements and publish current operational alerts
  • To meet child-safety, work-health-and-safety and record-keeping obligations

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it is needed to run the business:

  • Xero — our existing accounting and payroll system. The current website provides an audited payroll-readiness preview; staff records and approved hours will be sent only after the production OAuth connection, pay periods, staff mappings and export safeguards are completed.
  • Shopify — if and when the merchandise store opens, Shopify processes orders, payments and refunds under its own privacy terms. We do not store card numbers ourselves at any time.
  • Printify — if used to fulfil merchandise, receives the delivery details needed to send an order.
  • Open-Meteo — supplies public weather forecasts for Bendigo. No personal information is sent to it.
  • Meta / Facebook — supplies the optional Facebook timeline on the homepage. The connection is visitor-initiated and the full feed remains hosted by Meta.
  • Google or Apple — only when you choose that family account sign-in option. The provider verifies your identity and shares the limited account details described above under its own privacy terms.
  • Professional advisers, insurers and regulators — where we are required or permitted by law.

Integration boundary: Xero is used by the business, but the website-to-Xero connection is not active until production OAuth, staff mappings and export safeguards are configured. Shopify and Printify are not treated as live until their credentials, supplier approvals and checkout tests are complete. The production hosting provider and storage region must be documented before launch.

Storing and protecting information

Passwords are stored as PBKDF2 hashes and never in readable form. Sign-in sessions use HttpOnly, SameSite cookies and only a one-way digest of each session token is stored. Emergency contacts, allergies, medications, medical notes and support notes are encrypted before database storage and are revealed only after an authorised request. Cross-site request protection, sign-in rate limits and server-side role checks protect family, staff and management information. Google and Apple sign-in attempts use short-lived replay protection, and provider identity tokens are verified on the server. Integration credentials are never exposed to the browser.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach occurs that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected people and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

How long we keep it

General enquiries that do not lead to enrolment are kept for six months and are then scheduled for secure deletion. Staff operational records are kept until the end of the relevant financial year. A record is kept longer where Australian law, an insurer, child-safety obligations, an active dispute or another binding requirement requires it.

Enrolled-family, swimmer, achievement, financial and support-ticket records are kept only for their operational, safety and legal purpose. The final production retention schedule and automated deletion jobs must be approved before launch.

Children’s information

Most swimmers are children. We collect their information from a parent or guardian, not from the child, and we do not create accounts for children directly. A parent or guardian can see and correct everything we hold about their swimmer through their family account, or by contacting us.

Photographs and video are covered separately — see our photo and media consent. Consent is never assumed, is recorded per swimmer, and can be withdrawn at any time.

Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you or your swimmer, and ask us to correct anything that is wrong. Most details can be updated directly in your family account. For anything else, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable period.

If we cannot give you access, we will explain why in writing.

Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your personal information, email bendigo@hvswimschool.com. Paul or Laura Smith will investigate the complaint. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
