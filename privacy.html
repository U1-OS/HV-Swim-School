Australian Privacy Principles

Your information, and how we look after it.

What we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how you can access or correct it.

Last reviewed 28 August 2026 · Review status

HV Swim Bendigo ("we", "us") runs swimming lessons in California Gully, Bendigo, Victoria. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website and family, staff and management platform, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how you can access or correct it.

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

HV Swim to confirm: Registered business name and ABN, the postal address for privacy correspondence, and the named person or role responsible for privacy enquiries and complaints.

Information we collect

When you make an enquiry

Our enquiry form does not require an account. It collects the swimmer's first name and age range, their current confidence level and goal, the program and class times you are interested in, and your name, email address, phone number and preferred contact method. There are two optional free-text fields for lesson experience and support needs.

The support-needs field asks for teaching preferences rather than detailed medical history. Please do not enter clinical detail there — if a swimmer has a medical condition we need to know about, we will collect that properly at enrolment.

When you enrol a swimmer

A family account stores your name, email address, phone number and a securely hashed password. We never store your password itself.

For each swimmer we record their name, date of birth, current level, an emergency contact, any medical or support notes relevant to safe teaching, and whether photo and media consent has been given. Photo consent is off unless you turn it on.

Date of birth, emergency contacts and medical notes are collected because we teach children in water and need to place swimmers correctly, respond to incidents and reach a responsible adult quickly. Medical notes are health information and receive a higher level of protection under Australian privacy law. We collect them only with your consent and only to the extent they affect safe participation.

Bookings and lesson history

We record class bookings, cancellations, completed lessons and waitlist positions so we can run classes, manage capacity fairly and show you your own history.

If you work for us

For staff we hold rosters, timesheets, approved hours and qualification records including reference numbers and expiry dates, so we can meet supervision, first-aid and child-safety obligations.

Clock-in location. When a staff member clocks in or out, the platform records the latitude, longitude and accuracy of the device at that moment. This is used to confirm attendance at the correct venue and is visible to management. It is not continuous tracking — no location is recorded between clock-in and clock-out, or when the app is closed.

HV Swim to confirm: How long clock-in location records are kept, who may view them, and the wording of the separate staff notice explaining this before a staff member first clocks in.

Automatically collected information

When someone signs in, we log the email address used, the IP address it came from and whether the attempt succeeded. This protects accounts against password-guessing attacks. We keep sign-in records only as long as they are useful for that purpose.

Our public pages do not use advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to enquiries and match swimmers to suitable classes
  • To run lessons safely, including responding to incidents
  • To manage bookings, waitlists, cancellations and payments
  • To roster staff, record hours and meet employment and payroll obligations
  • To send information about your own lessons, classes and account
  • To meet child-safety, work-health-and-safety and record-keeping obligations

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it is needed to run the business:

  • Xero — our accounting and payroll system. Staff records, approved hours and timesheets are sent to Xero. Customer payment records may also be reconciled there.
  • Shopify — if and when the merchandise store opens, Shopify processes orders, payments and refunds under its own privacy terms. We do not store card numbers ourselves at any time.
  • Printify — if used to fulfil merchandise, receives the delivery details needed to send an order.
  • Open-Meteo — supplies public weather forecasts for Bendigo. No personal information is sent to it.
  • Professional advisers, insurers and regulators — where we are required or permitted by law.

HV Swim to confirm: Whether Shopify, Printify and Xero are connected at launch, and the hosting provider and country where the database will be stored. This policy assumes Australian hosting.

Storing and protecting information

Passwords are stored as PBKDF2 hashes and never in readable form. Sign-in sessions use HttpOnly cookies with cross-site request protection, sign-in attempts are rate limited, and access to family, staff and management information is restricted by role. Integration credentials are encrypted and are never exposed to the browser.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach occurs that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected people and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or as long as the law requires — employment and financial records in particular must be kept for set periods.

HV Swim to confirm: Retention periods for enquiries that do not lead to enrolment, swimmer records after a family leaves, staff records after employment ends, and clock-in location data.

Children’s information

Most swimmers are children. We collect their information from a parent or guardian, not from the child, and we do not create accounts for children directly. A parent or guardian can see and correct everything we hold about their swimmer through their family account, or by contacting us.

Photographs and video are covered separately — see our photo and media consent. Consent is never assumed, is recorded per swimmer, and can be withdrawn at any time.

Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you or your swimmer, and ask us to correct anything that is wrong. Most details can be updated directly in your family account. For anything else, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable period.

If we cannot give you access, we will explain why in writing.

Complaints

If you think we have mishandled your personal information, contact us first and we will investigate. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
