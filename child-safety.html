Child safety & speaking up

Clear information for families, visitors and the HV Swim community.

Updated 13 September 2026 · Prepared for review before launch

PrivacyCookiesSecurityAccessibilityTerms & cancellationsPhoto consentChild safety

Review status: This page provides practical help and verified external reporting routes. HV Swim’s full child-safety policy, staff code of conduct, named child-safety lead and independent internal contact still require owner approval before launch.

Something doesn’t feel right?

You can tell a trusted adult if something makes you feel worried, uncomfortable or unsafe. You do not have to find the perfect words or have proof before asking for help. If the first person does not help, tell another trusted adult.

Immediate danger

If someone is in immediate danger, call 000. A website enquiry is not an emergency service.

Talk with HV Swim

Children, parents and carers can call 0413 462 112 and ask to speak with management about a child-safety concern. Please avoid sending detailed incident or medical records through a public enquiry. Ask for a suitable private way to share them.

Information about a concern may need to be shared with appropriate people or authorities to protect a child. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

You can seek help outside the school

You do not need to raise a concern with HV Swim first. For concerns about an organisation’s Child Safe Standards, contact Victoria’s Social Services Regulator on 1300 310 778 or childsafeorgs@ssr.vic.gov.au. The regulator can help identify the appropriate reporting route.

For reporting options, including Child Protection and reportable conduct, use the Victorian Government’s reporting guidance.

A welcoming, respectful environment

Children should be heard and treated with dignity. Families can discuss cultural safety, disability access, communication needs and ways to help their child participate. The full operating policies must explain how these commitments are put into practice.

Photos and online information

Photo permission is separate from taking swimming lessons. Read the photo and media consent information. For detailed child-safety concerns, ask management or the relevant authority for a suitable confidential channel. Routine lesson-safety details can be updated in the family account.

Policy approval before launch

The owner must approve the child-safety policy, complaints procedure, staff code of conduct, screening and reporting arrangements, and named contacts. The review should address the Victorian Child Safe Standards. This page is not a certification of compliance.
