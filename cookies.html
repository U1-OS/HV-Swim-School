Cookies & your choices

Understand what this website saves on your device and control optional content.

Updated 13 September 2026

The current website uses necessary account cookies and small amounts of browser storage. It has no advertising pixels or third-party analytics. You can enquire and use the site without loading Facebook.

Necessary account cookie

After sign-in, an HttpOnly, SameSite Strict cookie keeps your account session secure for up to 14 days. Production uses HTTPS and the Secure flag. Signing out revokes the session and removes this cookie. Blocking it prevents sign-in; public pages remain available.

Choosing Google or Apple sign-in sets a separate HttpOnly security cookie for up to 10 minutes to bind that attempt to your browser. Apple uses SameSite None with Secure in production for its cross-site callback; Google uses SameSite Lax. The cookie is cleared on successful sign-in.

What is saved in your browser

  • Motion preference: remembers whether you have paused animation, until you clear it.
  • Weather and alert cache: saves public weather and the last alert shown. Weather data is treated as current for a limited time; clearing storage removes the cache.
  • Enquiry choices: your age, confidence, goal, program and day preferences may be kept in this tab while you complete the form. Names, contact details and free-text messages are not saved in this draft. It is removed when the enquiry succeeds or the tab session ends.
  • Merchandise basket: saves chosen products and a provider basket reference when shopping is enabled, until cleared. It does not hold card details.
  • Offline files: the service worker may cache public website files and selected public data. Protected account API responses are not cached.

Optional Facebook content

Facebook is disconnected by default. Choosing “Load Facebook posts” connects to Meta, which may receive your IP address and device information and use cookies under its privacy policy. This choice applies to the current page only. “Hide Facebook posts” removes the embedded feed; it cannot delete cookies Meta has already set or recall data already sent.

Clear saved preferences

This removes this website’s saved motion, weather, alert, enquiry and basket preferences. It does not delete your account, sign you out, erase Meta cookies, remove downloaded files or clear offline website files. Use your browser’s site-data settings to remove all site storage. Unsaved choices in other open tabs may be saved again; close those tabs first.

Changes and help

New analytics or advertising requires a fresh privacy assessment and appropriate choices before activation. Read our privacy policy or call 0413 462 112 for help.
