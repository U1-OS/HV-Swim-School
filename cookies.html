Cookies

Clear information for families, visitors and the HV Swim community.

Updated 13 September 2026 · Prepared for review before launch

The current website uses necessary account cookies and small amounts of browser storage. It has no advertising pixels or third-party analytics. No social feed is embedded in the public pages.

Necessary account cookie

After sign-in, an HttpOnly, SameSite Strict cookie keeps your account session secure for up to 14 days. Production configuration requires HTTPS and the Secure flag. Signing out revokes the session and removes this cookie. Blocking it prevents sign-in; public pages remain available.

When enabled, choosing Google or Apple sign-in sets a separate HttpOnly security cookie for up to 10 minutes to bind that attempt to your browser. Apple uses SameSite None with Secure in production for its cross-site callback; Google uses SameSite Lax. The cookie is cleared on successful sign-in.

What is saved in your browser

  • Motion preference: remembers whether you have paused animation, until you clear it.
  • Earlier versions: older weather and alert preferences may remain until cleared. The fresh public pages do not save these preferences.
  • Enquiry choices: your age, confidence, goal, program and day preferences may be kept in this tab while you complete the form. Names, contact details and free-text messages are not saved in this draft. It is removed when the enquiry succeeds or the tab session ends.
  • Merchandise basket: saves chosen products and a provider basket reference when shopping is enabled, until cleared. It does not hold card details.
  • Offline files: the service worker may cache public pages and website assets. Current availability, venue conditions, products and account API responses require a connection and are not served from an offline data cache.

External services

Facebook and map links open external websites only when you select them. Those services may use cookies and handle technical information under their own privacy terms. No Facebook feed loads inside our public pages.

Clear saved preferences

This removes this website’s saved motion, weather, alert, enquiry and basket preferences. It does not delete your account, sign you out, erase Meta cookies, remove downloaded files or clear cached pages and website assets. Use your browser’s site-data settings to remove all site storage. Unsaved choices in other open tabs may be saved again; close those tabs first.

Changes and help

New analytics or advertising requires a fresh privacy assessment and appropriate choices before activation. Read our privacy policy or call 0413 462 112 for help.
